HiraLearn teaches you all 104 hiragana: reading, writing and listening. It works like a calligraphy desk: every kana you learn goes from a faint outline to pencil, then to ink, until it earns its seal.

<b>Learn a few at a time</b>
Each lesson introduces up to five new kana. You see each one big, hear how it sounds and watch its stroke order. Then you practise it straight away: pick its reading, pick it by sound, trace it, type it and finally write it from memory.

<b>Spaced repetition that adapts to you</b>
Every kana gets separate cards for reading, writing and listening. A right answer pushes the next review further out, from hours to months. A wrong answer brings it back in ten minutes and asks it again later in the same session. Questions get harder as you get better: from multiple choice to typing, from picking a kana to drawing it.

<b>Write with your finger</b>
Your handwriting is checked stroke by stroke against reference stroke order: shape, number of strokes, order and direction. If you write a different kana, the app notices that too.

<b>It knows what you confuse</b>
Answer options are chosen from kana that look alike (あ/お/め/ぬ, さ/ち/き, ね/れ/わ…). If you mix two kana up, both come back soon. "Your confusions" shows the pairs you mix up most often, and a short drill puts them side by side.

<b>Read real words</b>
Once you know their kana, you can read about 170 everyday Japanese words.

<b>Also included</b>
• The complete hiragana: basic kana, dakuten, handakuten and combinations (きゃ, しゅ, ちょ…)
• A kana chart showing your progress
• An optional daily reminder when reviews are waiting
• Choose which question types you want
• Light and dark mode
• English, Hungarian and German

<b>Private and offline</b>
No account, no ads, no tracking. HiraLearn works without an internet connection and your progress stays on your phone. Sounds come from your phone's Japanese text-to-speech voice.
